## `b1` — earn the secret FIRE / ICE fight

A frame with `b1` marks the round as "secret earned". When the round ends, the game does not go on to the
next round or leave the fight: the screen fades out and a new match starts against a secret opponent.

- From a normal fight the next match is against **FIRE**; from the FIRE match it is against **ICE**; in
  the ICE match `b1` does nothing.
- The new match stays in the Fire Pit, which is redrawn in its fire or ice colours, the round score
  restarts at 0-0 and points are worth more.
- It only works in one- and two-player games. In a tournament the round ends normally.
- It counts once per round, however many `b1` frames are shown or however long they last. It works the
  same in a robot's own move and in an object the robot spawns.
- `b1` has no conditions of its own, so the move that carries it must be the gate. Every shipped `b1` is
  on a move that is only allowed when the Fire Pit secret is open: the Fire Pit arena, one player
  against the computer, not a tournament, and a high difficulty (the fifth level or above for FIRE, the
  hidden seventh level for ICE). Put `b1` on a move with that restriction, or any fight could jump to the
  secret opponents.
- It is often paired with `b2`, the other Fire Pit secret tag, on the same frame or the next one.
- Shipped use: the Fire Pit finishers of every robot except Nova, e.g. Jaguar's victory move 28
  (`…beaxb1I40-…`), Shredder's move 29 (`…beaxm55my+30b1b2s28l60E1-…`) and Chronos' move 36
  (`…bf60beb1A1-b1Z500-Z20`). Shadow's finisher has it in the object it spawns and in the move it
  ends with, and Electra's finisher ends by switching to her move 50, which has it. No arena or menu
  animation uses it.
