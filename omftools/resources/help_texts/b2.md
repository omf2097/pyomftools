## `b2` — break the Fire Pit floor grate

A frame with `b2` breaks the floor grate of the Fire Pit: a hole with flames showing through opens in the
front middle of the floor, behind the robots. It only works while the Fire Pit secret is open; anywhere
else the tag does nothing.

- The Fire Pit secret is open only in the Fire Pit arena, in a one- or two-player game against a
  computer opponent, not in a tournament, at a high difficulty (the fifth level or above, the seventh for
  the ICE match). This is the same condition the Fire Pit finishers' move restriction checks.
- Once broken, the grate stays broken for the rest of the match, including later rounds. It is whole
  again when a new match starts, including the secret FIRE or ICE match that `b1` starts.
- While the grate is broken, no scrap flies off either robot: the pieces thrown when a hit lands and
  every `m12` burst are skipped, so a robot destroyed over the hole seems to fall into the pit. An `m12`
  on the same frame as `b2` is already skipped.
- The hole appears one tick after the frame. Repeating the tag, or holding the frame, changes nothing.
- Whose string carries it does not matter: a robot's own move, the string a move gives the opponent, or
  an object the robot spawns all work the same.
- It is usually paired with `b1` (earn the secret FIRE / ICE match), on the same frame or with `b1` also
  on the frame before.
- Shipped use: the Fire Pit finishers, e.g. Shredder's move 29 (`…beaxm55my+30b1b2s28l60E1-…`),
  Gargoyle's victory move 33, the object Shadow's finisher spawns, and the string Electra's victory move
  30 gives the opponent. Jaguar's and Thorn's destruction moves also put `b2` in the victim's string, two
  frames before a large `m12` scrap burst: in a normal fight the tag does nothing and the scrap flies, with
  the Fire Pit secret open the grate breaks and the scrap is skipped. No arena or menu animation uses it.
